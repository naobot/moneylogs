audit · small screens (≤ 600px) · started 26 nov

quieter, clearer, still two inks

Keep the terminal look. Restore meaning to the levers it already has: frame weight, line style, inversion, texture, case. Fix the system first, then pass over each screen with one checklist.

diagnosis

01
One voice for everything.
Inversion, 2px frames and caps are used for structure and for state, so nothing stands out.
02
Em compounding.
Phone root 0.8 × component 0.7 × nested 0.85 pushes text under the micro floor (≈ 9px and below).
03
Density without tiers.
ui() scales every gap the same, so items within a group sit as close as groups to each other.
04
Always-on chrome.
Forms, strips and menus all stay expanded on a 390px screen. Nothing waits behind a toggle.

per-screen checklist

  • [ ] inverted blocks: active states only, 0 structural
  • [ ] frame weights in view: 2 at most
  • [ ] smallest text ≥ 11px rendered
  • [ ] every tap target ≥ 44px at UI size M
  • [ ] what is collapsed by default, and why
  • [ ] one display numeral at most
  • [ ] spacing: inside < between < group < section
