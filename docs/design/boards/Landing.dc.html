your spending, day to day

with friends, gently

  • Friends, not followersAdd people you actually know with a personal invite link. There's no public feed.
  • Share a logInvite a partner or flatmates into a financial log and track the household together.
  • Every entry is a postEach purchase is a little microblog post. Write a line or a paragraph about why you bought it, and add a photo if you like. Friends can react or leave a comment.
  • Group logsStart one for a trip. Particularly open friend groups can keep one going for a fond, slightly voyeuristic look at each other's lives. Extra fun if you live in different cities.
